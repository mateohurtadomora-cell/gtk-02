About GTK

Knowledge creates no value on its own: it creates value when it connects. Connecting is precisely our craft, and we have been practising it across two continents for over twenty years.

01Knowledge ecosystems

At Global Technology Knowledge Corp. we create knowledge ecosystems at local, regional, national and global levels. We reach full optimization through a well-balanced combination of knowledge engineering and the addition of the best suited agents and engines to each project and to each phase.

02Institutional alliances

We have firm alliances and agreements with some of the most prestigious research centers and higher education institutions in the world and with some of the most powerful high-tech companies in the educational sector.

03MECE strategiesSignature line

We emphasize our line of developments for multilevel association and partnership strategies MECE (Mutually-Exclusive/Collectively-Exhaustive) as an integral part of the added-value work conducted by Global Technology Knowledge for its strategic partners.

04Team and talent

Our team brings together highly specialized human talent, all of them experts in the creation, management, coordination, implementation and development of complex projects.

05Value creation

Global Technology Knowledge Corp. is specialized in creating and adding value associated to the integration of activities and intangible assets whose origins are intelligence and knowledge put into interactions with information and technology. These developments converge on the so-called Knowledge Society.

06Who works with us

We work alongside governments, regional communities, municipalities, multinational companies, universities, research centers, quality agencies and financial institutions and investors.

22@ Barcelona · b-Tec

The 22@ Knowledge City in Barcelona (Spain) through the b-Tec initiative (Barcelona Innovació Tecnològica) and the creation of its bridging strategy, the b-Tec summer sessions, with the collaboration, among other entities, of MIT, University of California and Columbia University, in the fields of energy and sustainability.

Academic partnerships02

The planning of alliances for the academic internationalization in the USA of Universidad San Pablo CEU. Universidad San Pablo CEU is the most important private academic institution in Spain.

BCNY Master's · UB + Columbia

ACAP · Comunidad de Madrid

The internationalization of the activities of the Agencia de Calidad, Acreditación y Prospectiva de la Comunidad de Madrid (ACAP). An agency of the Madrid regional government, reaching collaboration agreements with ABET, ABA and other US accrediting bodies.
